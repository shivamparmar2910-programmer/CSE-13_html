The Legend Bhagat Singh

Bhagat Singh was an Indian freedom fighter and revolutionary. He was born on 28 September 1907 in Banga, Punjab, British India. He is considered to be one of the most influential revolutionaries of the Indian independence movement. Bhagat Singh was involved in several acts of revolutionary activities against the British colonial rule, including the bombing of the Central Legislative Assembly in Delhi in 1929. He was arrested and later executed by hanging on 23 March 1931 at the age of 23. His courage, patriotism, and sacrifice have made him a symbol of resistance and a national hero in India.
